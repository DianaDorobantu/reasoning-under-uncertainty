## Overview

The objectives of this repo are to:

1. Understand the theoretical foundations of computational decision-making under uncertainty
2. Explore mathematical approaches to modelling uncertainty
3. Understand optimization and control methods for sequential decisions
4. Implement decision-making algorithms and computational models
5. Investigate the relationship between optimization, learning and decision-making under uncertainty
6. Apply these methods to practical problems in energy sector

Important areas include:

- Mathematical Optimization
- Markov Decision Processes (MDPs)
- Model Predictive Control (MPC)
- Stochastic Optimization
- Dynamic Programming
- Robust Optimization
- Game Theory
